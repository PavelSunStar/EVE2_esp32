## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-06

### Added

- ESP32 driver for BT815, BT816, and BT817 EVE graphics controllers.
- Single-SPI and quad-SPI transfers.
- Display-list and co-processor command helpers.
- VGA timing presets, RAM_G uploads, and buffered frame submission.
- Hello World and sprite examples.
- Processing 4 image converter.
- Installation, wiring, API, and contribution documentation.
- Automatic resource cleanup and protection against unsafe driver copies.
